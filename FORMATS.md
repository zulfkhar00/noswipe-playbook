# NoSwipe formats

Each format is an experiment. Run 2 episodes over 2–3 days, log the results in `FORMAT_LAB.md`, then double down, iterate the hook, or kill it.

---

## Format 1 · Your Dating App Receipt

**What it tests:** pain. "Swiping is costing me more than I thought."
**One-line premise:** a guy's dating-app year prints out as a long, itemized receipt, and the total is brutal. Then a tiny NoSwipe receipt prints instead.

### Why it works
- **Instantly clear:** everyone knows how a receipt works, so a cold viewer gets it in one second.
- **"That's literally me":** each line is a familiar dating-app pain (the "hey"s, the ghosting, the 1 AM swiping). That drives shares and comments.
- **A built-in reveal:** the total slams down as the punchline, and the second receipt is the relief.

### The narrator: the cashier
A deadpan cashier voice (Witty McGee) reads the receipt to him like a checkout clerk, with dry, warm jokes ("Will that be cash, or… your youth?"). The cashier is an obvious comedy character, not a customer testimonial, so nobody is claiming to be a NoSwipe user.

### Episode structure (target 30–40 s, hard max 45)
1. **Context + hook (0–3 s):** on-screen, plain: **"His dating app receipt for this year."** The phone prints a receipt out of its top like a thermal printer, and the first line is already visible on frame 1.
2. **The itemized lines (≈3–20 s):** 6–9 lines print one by one, with the cashier commenting on a few. The values sit in a right-hand column, and a running "TIME SPENT" figure grows. Every line is a recognisable dating-app pain, never a joke about women.
3. **The total (≈20–24 s):** a "TOTAL" line slams down in big type, plus one plain equivalent computed from the receipt's own numbers ("that's nine full days").
4. **The turn (≈24–33 s):** "Or… let NoSwipe do it." (always say the name). A short NoSwipe receipt prints beside it: "Tell us your type: 2 min · Profiles, swiping, chatting: us · Date booked: Sat 11 AM, coffee + walk, Maya · Your time: 2 minutes." Optionally the site's date card slides in. A soft "noswipe.app" pill appears.
5. **End card:** "Stop swiping. Just show up." with noswipe.app, ideally rhyming with frame 1 (a receipt) so it loops.

### What changes between episodes
- **The persona:** whose receipt it is (the gym guy, the busy founder, the over-texter, a summer on the apps, the guy who only swipes on his commute).
- **The receipt items and the equivalent:** e.g. hours turned into days or Netflix seasons, swipes turned into a distance walked. It must be arithmetic on the receipt's own numbers, never a real-world statistic.
- **The cashier's jokes and the NoSwipe receipt's example date:** use Maya, Jess or Priya from the site.

### Honesty rules for this format
- The receipt belongs to a fictional character. Never call its numbers "average", "typical" or "the average guy's". No real statistics unless sourced.
- The NoSwipe receipt shows one example date, as on the site, and never states a number of dates. No disclaimer line is needed.
- No $1, no app logos or UI. Tinder, Bumble and Hinge appear only as plain text in the brand font.

### Visual direction
- **The site's look in 2.5D:**
  - cream background with the lime and lilac blobs on far planes
  - the phone on the mid plane, acting as the printer
  - the receipt paper curling toward the camera on nearer planes, with soft shadows on the plane behind
- **The receipt:** warm white thermal paper with a zigzag torn edge. Item text in Figtree, totals and the TOTAL slam in Bagel Fat One. Grape ink, with coral for bad numbers and lime for the NoSwipe receipt.
- **Camera:** a slow tilt down following the paper as it prints, and one push-in on the TOTAL. The signature transition is `SwipeCuts`.
- **Sound:** thermal-printer chatter on each line, a "ka-ching" on the total, a paper tear for the turn, a sparkle ping on "Date booked".
- **Ad-safe layout:** nothing critical in the top 270 px or the bottom 670 px. Captions sit around y = 1150, and the receipt's key line is always inside the safe band.

### What to measure
In priority order: link clicks or profile visits (this is a demand test), % watched, shares and saves per 1,000 views, comments.

## Format 2 · Pre-Game

**What it tests:** identity. "That's my Friday night." (Secondary: curiosity, because a commentated night is a new way to watch a familiar pain.)
**One-line premise:** a guy's dating-app night plays out live in one room, commentated like a sports broadcast, with a scoreboard, replays and slow-mo. The score is brutal, then NoSwipe comes on as the substitution.

### Why it works
- **Scene-led, not a list:** about 70% is a visible mini story in one full-frame setting (his couch, a parked car, a kitchen) where he acts and the night escalates. The broadcast overlay frames the story and doesn't replace it.
- **Instantly clear, even for non-fans:** only universal broadcast cues: a scoreboard, a LIVE tag, replays, slow-mo and the commentator's sighs. No jargon.
- **Built-in jokes:** a replay of a tiny mistake, a telestrator circle around the "hey", and the score that never moves.
- **A natural turn:** "Substitution: NoSwipe." The new player takes the field and the scoreboard changes.

### The narrator: the play-by-play commentator
A single voice, Witty McGee, as a deadpan, warm broadcast commentator who calls the evening like a match and sighs a lot. He is an obvious comedy character, not a customer, so nobody is claiming to be a NoSwipe user. There is no second voice.

### Episode structure (target 30–40 s, hard max 45)
1. **Context + hook (0–3 s):** plain on-screen text, e.g. **"Live: his Friday night."** Frame 1 already shows the room, the guy mid-action, a LIVE tag and a scoreboard.
2. **The kickoff (≈3–8 s):** the commentator states the plan. The guy starts to act.
3. **The escalation (≈8–24 s):** 3–4 escalating moments in the same room, with time jumps on the scoreboard clock. One replay or slow-mo on a small failure, plus one telestrator circle or arrow. Every moment is a recognisable app pain, and the joke is on the apps or his evening, never on women.
4. **The final score (≈24–27 s):** the scoreboard lands its joke value and the commentator sighs.
5. **The turn (≈27–35 s):** "Substitution: NoSwipe." Plainly say what it does: builds the profiles, swipes, chats and books the date. The site's date card slides in (one example date) and a soft "noswipe.app" pill appears.
6. **Payoff + end card (≈35–40 s):** the same room, the scoreboard now reads differently. End card "Stop swiping. Just show up." with noswipe.app.

### What changes between episodes
- **The room and the persona:** couch, parked car, office desk, bed, kitchen table, train platform.
- **The night's escalation and the replay gag.**
- **Scoreboard labels and joke values:** e.g. "Date plans · Replies · Snacks left".
- **The example date:** Maya, Jess or Priya from the site, with a generic place.

### Honesty rules for this format
- Scoreboard values are jokes about one fictional evening, not statistics. Never call them average or typical.
- Never state or imply a number of dates. The NoSwipe moment shows one example date, as on the site.
- No $1, no app logos or UI. Tinder, Bumble and Hinge appear only as plain text, if at all.
- The commentator is a comedy character. No fake users, reviews or results.
- No real named places or teams. The joke is never on her.

### Visual direction (the format's own look, not the site's)
- **A prime-time sports broadcast:** deep navy, electric blue and scoreboard yellow, with broadcast red for LIVE/FINAL and green for IN. Heavy condensed italic type (Avenir Next Condensed). Graphics are slanted bars with a gradient and a glossy shine sweep (`src/pregame/look.tsx`).
- **The scene:** a dark apartment shot like TV footage. Shaded flat drawing with no ink outlines, a cool blue TV rim light, warm lamp pools, a window that goes from sunset to city night to morning. The guy is drawn in the same style (`src/pregame/guy.tsx`), not the brand's outlined character.
- **Broadcast wrapper:** score bug (LIVE · two joke stats · clock), camera ID label, lower-thirds, REPLAY / SLOW-MO tags with a cool tint, yellow glowing telestrator ink, a FINAL results bar, and an LED substitution board (OUT red, IN green). Captions are white condensed italic with the spoken word on a yellow slash.
- **Transition:** hard cuts hidden behind a broadcast stinger (blue and yellow streaks with a spinning badge; an "R" badge into replays), not the site's card flick.
- **The brand moment:** only the NoSwipe turn (phone at work, the site's date card), the noswipe.app pill and the end card switch to the noswipe.app look, so the substitution also reads as a change of world.
- **Camera:** slow push-ins during the night, a snap to the FINAL bar. Ad-safe: the score bug sits at y ≈ 300, captions around y = 1150.

### Sound
Soft crowd murmur that thins out as the night drags, a whistle on kickoff, a "bwoop" on the replay, a pen-scribble for the telestrator, a sad trombone-style slide on the final score, and a bright crowd cheer plus a sparkle ping on the date.

### What to measure
In priority order: link clicks or profile visits (this is a demand test), % watched, shares and saves per 1,000 views, comments (look for "that's me").
