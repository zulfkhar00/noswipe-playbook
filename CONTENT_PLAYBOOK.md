# Content playbook

What makes short reels in our niche get watched. The nightly run keeps this up to date from our own stats
(`FORMAT_LAB.md`, `nightly/LEDGER.json`) and the weekly niche outliers (`~/feedback/outliers.json`).

Tags: `ours` = shown by our own posts · `niche` = shown by outliers in our niche · `general` = common platform
knowledge, not yet tested by us. Promote a claim when evidence comes in; drop it when our numbers contradict it.

## Hook (first 1-2 s)
- Our skip rate is 41-59%: about half the viewers leave in the first seconds. The hook matters more than anything else. `ours`
- A plain, relatable first-person situation, readable in under a second, worked ("Across the Alley" part 1: 12.5k). `ours`
- Something moving and a clear tension or question in frame 1; no slow fade-in, no logo, no setup. `general`

## Watch time
- Average watch is 7-13 s on our posts: shorter cuts and an ending that loops into the start should help. `general`

## Spread
- Sends (DM shares) are among the strongest signals for reaching non-followers: make it "send this to the friend who does this". `general`
- Series with a cliffhanger bring people back for the next part. `general`
